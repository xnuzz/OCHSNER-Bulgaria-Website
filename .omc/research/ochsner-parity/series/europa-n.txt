 
HOT WATER HEAT PUMPS
 OCHSNER EUROPA N 
OCHSNER offers a comprehensive range of warm water heat pumps. Every customer will find the suitable solution that optimally meets their warm water needs with the lowest operating costs. The systems operate with minimal energy consumption and deliver water temperatures of up to 65 °C. They utilize heat from the ambient air for warm water preparation and make use of the energy from the installation room.

 Hot water independent of the central heating 
OCHSNER water heat pumps are precisely designed for the hot water needs of a household and therefore always operate with maximum efficiency. The heating system, which is oversized for pure hot water preparation, can remain switched off during the warm months. Due to the high hot water temperatures, the devices achieve output capacities that are significantly larger than the storage content. OCHSNER hot water heat pumps already reach up to 65 °C in pure heat pump operation even at low indoor temperatures. This ensures a hygienically flawless drinking hot water quality even without a heating rod. This can be activated in case of a temporarily very high hot water demand, for example, when guests are in the house.
 Additional Function Cooling and Dehumidifying 
By extracting heat from the installation room
the OCHSNER hot water heat pumps can "cool and dehumidify". The compact systems require little space and can therefore also be installed in a storage, utility, or laundry drying room.

 Easy installation, quick retrofitting 
The hot water heat pumps from OCHSNER are plug-and-play, very quick to install, and almost maintenance-free.
They can be easily combined with any other heating technology.
 Efficient use of PV self-consumption 
By combining with a photovoltaic system, the hot water heat pump pays off doubly: It increases the economically sensible self-consumption, and the hot water preparation is almost free of charge. Hot water heat pumps for combination with photovoltaics can be recognized by the designation as SmartGrid-capable or SG-ready.
 Intuitive. User-friendly, economical: The OCHSNER hot water APP.
Optimize energy consumption without sacrificing comfort: The OCHSNER EASY App allows extensive settings to be made easily and intuitively on a smartphone. Individual time programs can be stored. Spontaneous or temporary adjustments are also possible – for example, when going on vacation or returning home earlier than expected. The current mixed water quantity can be read via the app. Important status information is sent to the user via push notification.  Frequently asked questions about OCHSNER EUROPA
 What is the EUROPA N designed for – and what is it not designed for?
The EUROPA N is exclusively designed for the preparation of hot water – it replaces or supplements the hot water function of an existing heating system, but does not provide space heating. The heat source is the room air at the installation location.
 Can the EUROPA N be operated independently of the central heating system?
Yes, the system operates completely autonomously. The heating system can remain switched off during the warm months – the hot water supply continues independently. Only the drinking water connections need to be adjusted for separation from an existing heating system.
 What hot water temperatures does the EUROPA N achieve?
In pure heat pump operation, the EUROPA N reaches up to 65 °C – even at low indoor temperatures. This ensures hygienically impeccable drinking water quality without a heating element. In case of a temporarily very high demand – for example, with guests in the house – a heating element can be activated.
 How much hot water is available?
The series is available in three storage sizes: 267, 393, and 415 liters. Due to high output capacities, effectively more hot water is available than the pure storage content would suggest.
 Where can the EUROPA N be installed?
The compact systems require little space and can also be installed in utility rooms, storage rooms, or laundry drying rooms. The OCHSNER system partner will clarify which area in the building is best suited during the on-site appointment.
 How complicated is the installation?
The EUROPA N models are plug-and-play and can be installed very quickly. They can be easily combined with any other heating technology and are virtually maintenance-free.
 Can the EUROPA N be combined with a photovoltaic system?
Yes. All models are SG-ready and have an integrated interface for using PV self-consumption. The hot water preparation can thus be specifically scheduled during times of high solar yield – significantly reducing operating costs.
