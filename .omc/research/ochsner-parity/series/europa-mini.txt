 
HOT WATER HEAT PUMPS
 OCHSNER EUROPA MINI 
OCHSNER offers a comprehensive range of hot water heat pumps. Every customer will find the suitable solution that optimally meets their hot water needs with the lowest operating costs. The systems operate with minimal energy input and deliver water temperatures of up to 60 °C. They utilize heat from the ambient air for hot water preparation and make use of the energy from the installation room.

 Hot water independent of the central heating 
OCHSNER water heat pumps are precisely designed for the hot water needs of a household and therefore always operate with maximum efficiency. The heating system, which is oversized for pure hot water preparation, can remain switched off during the warm months. Due to the high hot water temperatures, the devices achieve high output capacities. EUROPA MINI water heat pumps reach up to 60 °C in pure heat pump operation even at low indoor temperatures. This ensures hygienically perfect drinking hot water quality even without a heating rod. This can be activated in case of a temporarily very high hot water demand, for example, when guests are in the house.
 Additional function cooling and dehumidifying 
By extracting heat from the installation room, OCHSNER water heat pumps can "cool and dehumidify". The compact systems have a small footprint and can therefore also be installed in a storage, utility, or laundry drying room.

Save electricity in hot water generation
OCHSNER water heat pumps utilize waste heat and provide warm water highly efficiently and with low electricity consumption through the thermodynamic process. The OCHSNER technology thus makes a significant contribution to reducing energy costs and protecting the climate.

 Easy installation, quick retrofitting 
The water heat pumps from OCHSNER are plug-and-play, very quick to install, and almost maintenance-free.
They can be easily combined with any other heating technology.
 Efficient use of PV self-consumption 
By combining with a photovoltaic system, the water heat pump pays off doubly: it increases the economically sensible self-consumption, and the hot water preparation is almost free of charge. Water heat pumps for combination with photovoltaics are identified by the designation as SmartGrid-capable or SG-ready.

 Frequently asked questions about OCHSNER EUROPA
 What is the EUROPA MINI designed for?
The EUROPA MINI is a standalone Warmwasser-Wärmepumpe – not a heating system. It prepares warm water based on the ambient air at the installation site and complements or replaces the warm water function of an existing heating system. In combination with the existing warm water storage tank, the operating time of the heating system is reduced to the actual heating period – saving energy and increasing its lifespan.
 What warm water temperature does the EUROPA MINI reach?
In pure heat pump operation, the EUROPA MINI reaches up to 60 °C – even at low indoor temperatures. This ensures hygienically flawless drinking water quality without a heating element. In case of a temporarily very high demand, a heating element can be activated.
 Is the EUROPA MINI suitable for larger warm water needs?
Yes. Two or more units can be connected in combination with an external warm water storage tank – for residential buildings with multiple parties or commercial enterprises with high consumption, such as hair salons.
 Does the EUROPA MINI have a cooling function?
As a side effect, yes. Because the system extracts heat from the ambient air, it cools and dehumidifies the installation room – with the practical side benefit that laundry drying in the installation room is accelerated.
 How is the EUROPA MINI controlled?
Depending on the model, two variants are available: The IWPL model is operated via Tiptronic light. The IWP model features Tiptronic plus S with a touch display and is additionally SG-ready for the use of PV self-consumption.
 Are there any approval requirements or subsidies?
There are no approval requirements for the operation of a warm water heat pump. Subsidies may be possible as part of a heating renovation – details are provided by the OCHSNER funding information.
