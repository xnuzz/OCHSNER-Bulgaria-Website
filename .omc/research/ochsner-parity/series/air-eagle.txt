 OCHSNER AIR EAGLE 
Strong performance, low operating costs: The air/water heat pump OCHSNER AIR EAGLE with R290 technology guarantees reliable heat. Whether for new buildings or renovation of single-family and multi-family homes or commercial buildings - the powerful monoblock heat pump operates highly efficiently, even with existing radiator systems.
* The natural refrigerant R290 used has a minimal GWP (Global Warming Potential) of less than 1.

 Very high flow temperatures for all building types 
The OCHSNER AIR EAGLE delivers flow temperatures of up to 70 °C for heating and hot water. It is therefore suitable for both underfloor heating and conventional radiators. Optionally, it can be used for active cooling.
 Monoblock with the highest safety standards
The entire heat pump technology, including table evaporator and compressor, is integrated into the externally installed monoblock heat pump. This saves space in the house. Two water-carrying lines lead into the building. The OCHSNER R290 safety concept includes a system separation via a brine intermediate circuit. This separation of the refrigerant and heating circuit prevents the refrigerant from ever entering the heating water. This factory precaution is a unique feature of OCHSNER and ensures absolute operational safety.

 High-end electronics and app 
The OCHSNER AIR EAGLE is equipped with the advanced control OCHSNER TRONIC SMART (OTS). It allows user-friendly and versatile control via touch display or the OCHSNER Smart App independent of location via the internet.
 For larger new and existing buildings 
The OCHSNER AIR EAGLE high-tech heat pump is versatile in larger existing and new buildings such as single-family and multi-family houses, commercial, administration, etc. With very high flow temperatures of up to 70 °C, it is also suitable for radiators. For high heating loads, the series can be cascaded multiple times.
 Heating, hot water & cooling 
In addition to the main applications of heating and hot water, the AIR EAGLE models can also be optionally used for active cooling via underfloor heating or fan coils.

 Frequently Asked Questions about the OCHSNER AIR EAGLE
 How is the AIR EAGLE constructed?
The AIR EAGLE is a monoblock heat pump: all heat pump technology, including the evaporator and compressor, is integrated into the outdoor unit. Only two water-carrying lines lead into the building. A separate indoor unit – control module or hydro module – takes care of regulation and hydraulics.
 What is the brine intermediate circuit and what is its purpose?
The OCHSNER R290 safety concept completely separates the refrigerant and heating circuits through a factory-integrated brine intermediate circuit. This ensures that R290 can never enter the heating water under any circumstances. This system separation is a unique feature of OCHSNER and ensures absolute operational safety when using the natural refrigerant.
 Why does OCHSNER use R290 in the AIR EAGLE?
R290 (propane) is a natural refrigerant with a GWP (Global Warming Potential) of well below 1 – climatically nearly neutral and not affected by the EU F-Gas Regulation. It contains no halogenated substances and is not subject to any future usage restrictions.
 What flow temperatures does the AIR EAGLE achieve?
The AIR EAGLE delivers flow temperatures of up to 70 °C for heating and hot water. This makes it suitable for older buildings with radiators without the need for extensive adjustments to the existing heating system.
 How is the AIR EAGLE controlled?
The AIR EAGLE is equipped with the OCHSNER TRONIC SMART (OTS). Operation can be done either via the touch display on the indoor unit or location-independently via the OCHSNER Smart App. SG-Ready is integrated as standard.
