 OCHSNER AIR EAGLE 1830 CM 
 18 - 30 kW
 (CM1)
The heat pump works with a speed controlled compressor and delivers heat and DHW up to 70°C. It can also be used for energy saving surface cooling and for active air conditioning and room dehumidification via fan coils. The AIR EAGLE 1830 impresses with an unrivalled SCOP (5.3 at 35°C flow temperature). The controller and user interface are housed in the compact wall mounted indoor unit. The OCHSNER R290 safety concept includes system separation by means of a brine-filled intermediate circuit. This separation of the refrigerant circuit and heating circuit ensures maximum operational reliability and dependable protection of the heating system. It is controlled with the leading-edge Ochsner Tronic Smart (OTS) and can be operated either on the 7" touchscreen of the heat pump indoor unit or using the OCHSNER Smart app.
