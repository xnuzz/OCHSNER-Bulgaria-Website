 
 Hot water
 OCHSNER EUROPA N 300 CA 
For efficient DHW heating, separated from central heating, OCHSNER recommends the EUROPA series of hot water heat pumps. They work just as well teamed up with a boiler as with a solar thermal system, for example. OCHSNER exclusively offers hot water heat pumps either as compact appliances with an integrated 300 or 250 litre DHW tank or as split appliances – for external tanks with up to 500 litres capacity (larger households, commercial enterprises). Enjoy practical additional benefits such as storage room cooling or laundry room drying!
