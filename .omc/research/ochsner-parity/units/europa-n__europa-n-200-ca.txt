 
 Hot water
 OCHSNER EUROPA N 200 CA 
The integral DHW tank of the compact hot water heat pump has a capacity of 200 litres and delivers a usable volume of water of up to 267 litres for households of up to 4 people. The fully wired appliance is quick to install and has a footprint of less than half a square metre. Due to its low height, it is ideal for basement rooms with low ceilings.
