 
 Hot water
 OCHSNER EUROPA MINI IWP 
The split appliance generates DHW up to 60°C for external tanks with up to 500 litres capacity and is suitable for households of up to 5 people. The fully wired and compact appliance is quick to install. It can be easily combined with existing pellet boilers, solar thermal or conventional boiler systems. The model can be combined with a 300 or 500 litre tank, or as a twin solution with 750 l.
