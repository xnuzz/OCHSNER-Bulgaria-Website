 OCHSNER AIR MILAN 1016 T200 
 10 - 17 kW
 (T200)
The heat pump is suitable for building heat loads between 10 and 17 kW. It delivers flow temperatures up to 70°C. It can also be configured for active cooling. The slimline MULTI TOWER indoor unit has a footprint of less than half a square metre. It includes the heat pump control unit, a 100 litre buffer tank for heating and cooling, a 168 litre DHW tank and all hydraulic components.
