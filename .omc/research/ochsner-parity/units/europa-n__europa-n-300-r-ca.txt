 
 Hot water
 OCHSNER EUROPA N 300 R CA 
The integral DHW tank of the compact hot water heat pump has a capacity of 294 litres and delivers a usable volume of water of up to 393 litres for households of up to 5 people. A coil can be used to also connect a solar thermal system or boiler. The fully wired appliance is quick to install and has a footprint of less than half a square metre.
