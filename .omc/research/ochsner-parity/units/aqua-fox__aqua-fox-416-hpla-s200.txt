The heat pump works with a speed controlled compressor and delivers efficient heat and DHW up to 65°C. As an option, the heat pump can also be configured for passive cooling. The slimline indoor unit houses a 178 litre DHW tank made from stainless steel and requires less than half a square metre of floor space. It is controlled with the Ochsner Tronic Fox (OTF) and can be operated either on the heat pump touchscreen or using the OCHSNER Easy app. An intermediate heat exchanger (OCHSNER accessories) is required for system separation when using groundwater as a heat source.
