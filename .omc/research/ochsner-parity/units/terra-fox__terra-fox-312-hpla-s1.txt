The heat pump is suitable for building heat loads between 6 and 11 kW. It works with a speed controlled compressor and delivers heat and DHW up to 65°C. As an option, the heat pump can also be configured for passive cooling. The slimline indoor unit has a footprint of less than half a square metre. The heat pump is equipped with a climate friendly refrigerant.
