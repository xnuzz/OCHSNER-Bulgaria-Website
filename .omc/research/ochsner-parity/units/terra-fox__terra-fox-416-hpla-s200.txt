 10 - 16 kW
 (S200)
The heat pump is suitable for building heat loads between 10 and 16 kW. It works with a speed controlled compressor and delivers heat and DHW up to 65°C. As an option, the heat pump can also be configured for passive cooling. The slimline indoor unit houses a 178 litre DHW tank made from stainless steel and requires less than half a square metre of floor space. The heat pump is equipped with a climate friendly refrigerant.
